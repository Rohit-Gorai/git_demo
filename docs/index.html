What is Finance

Estimated time: 12 minutes

Learning Objectives

  • Explain finance in plain English.
  • Connect money decisions to risk and return.
  • Practice the concept in a spreadsheet-style sandbox.

Simple Explanation

Imagine you own a lemonade stand. Finance helps you decide how much cash you need, what to spend, what to save, and whether a decision is worth the risk.

Formula

Value today + decisions about risk and return

Time Value of Money

Money today can be invested, so it is usually worth more than the same amount in the future.

Future Value = Present Value × (1 + Rate)^Periods

Income Statement Basics

The income statement shows revenue, expenses, and profit over a period.

Revenue - Expenses = Net Income

Balance Sheet Basics

The balance sheet shows what a company owns and how those assets are funded.

Assets = Liabilities + Equity

Cash Flow Basics

Cash flow explains how cash moved through operating, investing, and financing activities.

Beginning Cash + Cash Inflows - Cash Outflows = Ending Cash

Excel Basics

Financial models separate assumptions, calculations, and outputs so decisions are easy to audit.
